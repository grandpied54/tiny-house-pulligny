The setting: garden, river and fishing

The garden runs straight down to the Madon river. Swans and plenty of birds are regular visitors; you can fish from the bank, read under the big oak tree and listen to nothing but nature. No neighbours overlooking you, no city noise.

  • Direct river access from the garden
  • Bank fishing (fishing licence required)
  • Garden furniture and quiet corners
  • Private on-site parking for cars and motorbikes

The ideal stopover on the way south

Driving from the Netherlands, Germany, Belgium or Luxembourg towards the Mediterranean? Pulligny is just minutes from the Metz–Nancy–Dijon route. Park in peace, sleep by the water and set off rested the next morning.

  • Private, secure parking
  • Late arrivals welcome
  • Fast Wi-Fi

For business travellers

20 minutes from Nancy and close to Neuves-Maisons, Toul and Lunéville. The Studio has a desk; both rentals have fast Wi-Fi and a fully equipped kitchen for longer stays.

Frequently asked questions

Can I fish on site?

Yes, the garden opens directly onto the Madon river. You need a fishing licence valid in Meurthe-et-Moselle, available online at cartedepeche.fr.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is private parking on site for cars and motorbikes.

Can I arrive late in the evening?

Yes, late arrivals are possible. Just let us know your arrival time.

How do I book?

Book on Airbnb or Booking.com using the buttons on each rental page, or contact us directly by phone or e-mail.
